Unthread

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026 · Developer: Edswitc tech

In short: Unthread keeps your game progress on your device. The game itself does not ask for, collect or sell your personal information. Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Who we are

Unthread (package com.lumenforge.unthread.pathpuzzle) is published on Google Play by Edswitc tech. For any privacy question, contact ahmadcowork6@gmail.com.

Data the game stores on your device

Your unlocked levels, completed levels, statistics and settings (sound, vibration, theme) are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or using Reset progress in Settings deletes them.

No account, no personal information

Unthread has no sign-in and no account. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unthread shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate (city- or country-level) location
  • Ad interactions, such as ad impressions and taps
  • Crash and diagnostic data related to the ad SDK

This data is sent over an encrypted connection. Google's use of it is governed by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). On first launch in these regions the game shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the game.
  • Personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, Google's US states message lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising.

Children

Unthread is not directed at children under 13 and is listed on Google Play for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have a concern, contact us at the address above.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app or reset progress. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
